### About

I've spent over a decade building businesses in international trade, exports, sourcing and distribution.

Most of what I know I learned on the ground: finding suppliers, negotiating procurement, winning customers, handling international orders and opening markets from scratch. My strength is commercial execution. I look for where the demand is, work out whether the economics hold up, put the right supply network in place, and get the product to market profitably.

I've worked across fashion exports, sourcing, logistics, exhibitions and a few new business ideas along the way. Those experiences have shaped how I think about growth, capital and execution.
